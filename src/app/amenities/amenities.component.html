Community Pools

Mill Creek has two community pools for residents in good standing—those who are current on dues and have no outstanding violation fines.

Locations: The Manor: 100 Oak Haven Drive
The Falls: 118 Mill Creek Drive
Season: Opens Mother's Day weekend
Hours: 6 a.m.–10 p.m., daily

Tennis Courts

Tennis courts at The Falls are available on a first-come basis—no reservation required. Please share the courts fairly and be considerate of other players.

Location: The Falls
Access: A valid pool key is required
Hours: 6 a.m.–10 p.m., daily

Basketball Courts

Basketball courts at The Manor are available on a first-come basis—no reservation required. Please share the courts fairly and be considerate of other players.

Location: The Manor
Access: A valid pool key is required
Hours: 6 a.m.–10 p.m., daily

Playgrounds

Neighborhood playgrounds with slides, climbing structures, and open play areas for residents and their families.

Location: Mill Creek community areas
Access: Open access
  • In April 2021, Governor Kemp lifted most requirements for COVID-19, including those for community swimming pools. However, Mill Creek residents are still encouraged to follow recommended precautions, including washing hands frequently in the bathrooms and maintaining a safe distance from others.
  • Mill Creek residents may allow guests into the pool areas, but residents must remain with their guests while they are using the pool amenities.
  • There are no longer any restrictions on bringing balls, frisbees, NERF toys, water noodles, pool floats, diving toys, or swim toys in general. However, residents who bring toys into the pool areas are responsible for removing them, or the toys will be discarded without notice.
  • Mill Creek residents may host their own birthday parties or swim parties, but must clean up any decorations—especially balloons—and dispose of food and drink items. No glitter, nail polish, or paints of any kind are allowed in the pool areas at any time. Pools cannot be reserved for exclusive use for a resident birthday or swim party unless the party is hosted by the Mill Creek Social Events Committee.
  • Any Mill Creek resident caught on camera holding or propping open a pool gate to allow others in without a pool key will be subject to a $200 fine and a 60-day suspension of amenity privileges, including deactivation of their pool keys.
  • Parents and guardians must ensure that infants or children using the pool do not have a diarrheal illness and wear swim diapers at all times while in the pool deck area.
  • All infants and children who are not toilet trained, and incontinent individuals, must wear swim diapers and plastic swim pants at all times. Violators of this rule will be fined for pool shock treatment and cleaning fees.
  • No glass containers of any kind—such as beer or soda bottles—sharp objects, or hazardous materials are allowed in or around pool areas. Violators will be held responsible for glass vacuuming and pool filling fees to restore the pools to a safe, bare-feet condition.
  • No animals, including dogs and cats, other than service animals, are allowed in the pool areas at any time. Violators will be removed from the pool area and lose their pool privileges for 30 days for each violation.
  • Shower and rinse thoroughly before entering the pool to remove body oils, sweat, beach sand, and other materials. This is especially important if you are coming from a local beach or nearby lake.
  • No food or drink is allowed within five feet of the pool water.
  • Children under the age of 14 must be accompanied by an adult, or they will be required to leave the pool areas immediately.
  • Swimmers with open wounds, skin conditions, or any other communicable conditions are not allowed in the pool water.
  • Swimming alone is not encouraged. Swim at your own risk.
  • Swimmers shall wear proper bathing suit attire at all times. No skinny-dipping is permitted at any time.
  • Spitting, spouting, or blowing one's nose into the pool water is not permitted.
  • Running or rough play in the pool areas is not permitted.
  • The pools are open from 6 a.m. until 10 p.m. each day. Being inside any of the pool areas before 6 a.m. or after 10 p.m. constitutes trespassing, unless express permission is granted by the Board of Directors.
  • The maximum number of swimmers allowed in each pool area is 300 people.
  • A first aid kit is available at each pool house for minor medical emergencies that may occur in or around the pool areas.
  • An emergency phone is located in the hallway of the pool house. Please do not take this phone off the hook and leave the handset dangling.
  • The certified pool operator at this facility is the Mill Creek Community Association. There are no posted lifeguards, so swimmers swim at their own personal risk and safety.
  • Basketball and tennis courts are intended for Mill Creek residents and their guests for basketball and tennis play only. All other activities for which the courts are not designed are strictly prohibited.
  • No animals, including dogs and cats, other than service animals, are permitted on the courts at any time.
  • Appropriate court shoes must be worn at all times on the courts. Cleats and spikes are strictly prohibited, as they can do major damage to the court surfaces.
  • Children under the age of 12 must be accompanied by an adult, or they will be removed from the courts immediately.
  • Please be respectful of other players on the courts. Loud music and heckling of other players are not permitted.
  • The following activities are strictly forbidden on the courts. Anyone caught violating this rule is subject to losing court privileges at the discretion of the Board of Directors and may be fined for damages to the courts, nets, and equipment:
  • Skateboards, bicycles, roller blades, roller skates, or any other activities that damage the court surface. Violators will be immediately asked to leave the courts.
  • Other sports equipment not intended for basketball or tennis play.
  • Birthday parties, social mixers, or food displays on the courts at any time.
  • Magic markers, crayons, spray paint, or other damaging ink instruments. Anyone caught vandalizing the courts will be fined and prosecuted to the fullest extent that Cherokee County law allows.
